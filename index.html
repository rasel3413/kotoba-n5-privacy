Overview

Kotoba N5 is a Japanese vocabulary flashcard app for the Minna no Nihongo Lessons 1–25 (JLPT N5) word set. The app works fully offline and does not require an account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress — which words you answered correctly or incorrectly, your mastery level per word, and your app settings — is stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. It is never sent to the developer or to any server, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the in-app "Reset all progress" option.

Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

Pronunciation playback uses the text-to-speech service installed on your device (for example, Google Text-to-Speech). The Japanese word being spoken is passed to that on-device service. Its handling of data is governed by the privacy policy of your device's speech service provider.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general app-usage information, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google AdMob terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Information we collect

The developer does not collect, store, or share any personal information through this app. The app contains no analytics or tracking code of its own.

Children

The app is a general educational tool and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, social, or user-generated content features.
